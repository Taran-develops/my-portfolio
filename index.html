Hi, I am Taran

I guess you could say I’m the kind of person who gets lost in the details. Whether I’m writing code or dreaming up new ideas, I’m always searching for that perfect combination of logic and beauty. I don't mind getting a little obsessed with the process — after all, the best things come from the most careful work.

I’m fascinated by technology. But not just for the sake of it. It’s the way we use it to connect, solve problems, and push boundaries that really excites me. I like to think of myself as someone who builds quietly, in the background, crafting digital spaces and experiences that people don't even realize they need — until they do.

I’m not here to shout about my accomplishments. Instead, I’m more interested in what you can build, what stories we can share, and how we can all move forward, one line of code at a time.

If you're curious, reach out. I'm always looking for new ideas, new challenges, and a little bit of intrigue.
